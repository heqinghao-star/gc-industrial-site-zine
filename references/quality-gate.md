# Quality Gate

Inspect the actual raster at full view and thumbnail size.

## Structure

- The requested ratio is correct; default is landscape 4:3.
- The result reads as a paper field with asymmetric open space.
- One coherent analytical event dominates.
- The target remains recognizable through declared invariants.
- The photograph has been structurally dismantled; it is not a large colorful photo with decorative lines placed on top.
- Fragments, dimensions, axes, hatches, and labels all explain the chosen structural thesis.
- No copied reference composition, accent shape, wording, interface, brand, signature, or watermark appears.

## Color

- Photo fragments are near-monochrome or materially subdued.
- One accent hue is visible at thumbnail size but remains small, normally 1–3% of the canvas.
- The accent comes from an observed target color or a defensible material/light relationship.
- The response reports the accent color and its source relationship.
- No default cobalt or repeated series color appears without target-specific justification.
- A dominant source field such as blue sky or golden foliage has not remained strongly saturated unless the user requested it.

## Typography and marks

- Default output contains only concise English Latin-alphabet text unless another language was requested.
- The title and labels are readable enough to function.
- No invented measurements, decorative coordinates, generic travel icons, or meaningless engineering terms appear.
- Marks are restrained and do not form a generic technical-overlay template.

## Reproduction

- Paper fiber, halftone, xerox softness, graphite, ink bleed, or slight misregistration is visible.
- The result remains flat and scanned rather than glossy, cinematic, or three-dimensional.

If a central check fails, revise once. Prioritize subject preservation, meaningful deconstruction, source separation, photo desaturation, and accent justification in that order. If the second result still fails, return the stronger version and state the remaining limitation.
